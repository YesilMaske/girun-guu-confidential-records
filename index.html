02 / Incident

A sudden spike in cursed energy was reported in a residential area. A Grade 1 sorcerer was sent to check it out and followed the trail to a house that was already on fire.

Inside, the boy was found unconscious near several bodies. A green mask was stuck to his face. The object was later classified as a Cursed Energy Booster.

The area around the house was heavily damaged. Curse activity had clearly taken place, but the exact number of curses and total casualties could not be confirmed at the time.

The boy was pulled out and taken for emergency treatment.

The object was supposed to increase the wearer’s cursed energy. The problem is that Haruki showed no signs of having any cursed energy of his own before this happened.

When he put the mask on, it released a massive amount of energy. Investigators believe this surge is what drew the curses to the area. Why the object reacted this way is still unclear.

We still don’t know who made it, who owned it before, or how the boy got his hands on it.

04 / Medical & Follow-Up Records

Haruki was unconscious when found. After he woke up at the facility, he remembered what happened and tried to tear the mask off his face. Staff had to restrain him.

He received psychological treatment for about two years.

At fifteen, on his doctor’s advice, he chose the name Girun-Guu and enrolled at Lemonhead Jujutsu High.

Available records do not clearly show the long-term effects of the incident.

05 / Outstanding Questions

  • Why did the object react so strongly to someone with no confirmed cursed energy?
  • Was the energy surge a defensive reaction, a malfunction, or something else entirely?
  • Did the surge directly cause the curse outbreak?
  • Who had the object before, and how did Haruki obtain it?

There is currently not enough evidence to place full responsibility on the subject. Further investigation into the object itself is recommended.
